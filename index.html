2. Corporate Tax Calculator

Estimated Corporate Tax: AED 0.00

General calculation: 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above. Small Business Relief may be available to eligible resident persons with revenue not exceeding AED 3 million for tax periods ending on or before 31 Dec 2029. Conditions and exclusions apply, including restrictions for Qualifying Free Zone Persons and certain MNE groups. Confirm eligibility for the entity and prior periods. Qualifying Free Zone Person treatment is not calculated here.
